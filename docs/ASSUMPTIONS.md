# Assumptions

- The only confirmed business fact is the name BR Tours and Travels.
- English, INR and Asia/Kolkata are configurable development defaults, not
  evidence of an office location or offered service region.
- The owner-supplied raster is approved as the current logo; the exact crop is
  used and the enhanced alternative is retained separately.
- Demo routes, dates and prices are synthetic and visible only in explicit
  development demo mode.
- The product is enquiry-led. Online payment, live inventory and customer
  accounts are out of current scope.
- Separate npm projects are an explicit owner override of the prompt pack's pnpm
  monorepo recommendation.
